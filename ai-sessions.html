In every session

Right-sized AI use

A few minutes of every session go to using the right amount of AI for the job. Everyday text chat has a small energy footprint. Most of the energy goes to video generation, always-on reasoning modes, models bigger than the task needs, and automations left running unattended. Those are often where the bill goes too.

Scope

What these sessions are, and what they aren't

Sessions are educational and operational. They are not legal, tax, or financial advice. In particular, I don't:

  • tell you whether a law applies to you, or sign off that a policy is compliant
  • write customer-facing privacy notices or contract terms
  • advise on AI used in hiring, lending, or tenant screening
  • build custom automations or work in your systems outside a session
  • give counseling. If a family conversation raises mental-health concerns, I'll point you to someone qualified

If something needs a lawyer, I'll tell you, and you'll go to them with a clear list of questions.
